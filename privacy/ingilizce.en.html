Ders Ders İngilizce — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

Ders Ders İngilizce does not collect personal data, does not create accounts, does not track users and does not show ads. Your progress stays on your device.

Data collected

The app collects no personal data and shares nothing with third parties. It does not create accounts or ask you to sign in. It uses no third-party advertising or analytics tools.

Progress and settings

Completed lessons, test results, your review schedule, the name you enter and settings such as the theme are stored only in your device's own storage. They are never sent to a server. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Read-aloud

English examples are spoken by your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

Speaking practice (microphone)

The microphone is turned on only in the Speaking exercise or when you tap the microphone button on a question, and only while you say that sentence; your phone asks for microphone permission first. If you decline, the rest of the app works as before. Speech recognition, which turns what you say into text, is your phone's own speech recognition service (Google on most phones); audio may be sent to that service's servers and is subject to its own privacy terms. The app does not record, store or send us any audio; it only compares the recognized text with the correct sentence at that moment and saves the sentence's best score to your progress on your device.

Daily reminder (notifications)

If you want, the app shows one short reminder notification a day; your phone asks for notification permission first. The reminders are scheduled by your phone's own scheduler. If you did not pick a time, the app chooses morning, noon or evening from the study records on your device. This happens only on your device; no data is sent. You can turn the reminder off in Settings at any time.

Network access

All lesson content ships inside the app. On launch the app downloads a single static configuration file to learn the minimum supported version. No personal data is sent with this request. Without an internet connection the app keeps working normally.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that collects data is ever added, this page and the Google Play data safety declaration will be updated accordingly.
