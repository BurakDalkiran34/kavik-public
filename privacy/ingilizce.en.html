Ders Ders İngilizce — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Summary

Ders Ders İngilizce does not collect personal data, does not create accounts, does not track users and does not show ads. Your progress stays on your device.

Data collected

The app collects no personal data and shares nothing with third parties. It does not create accounts or ask you to sign in. It uses no third-party advertising or analytics tools.

Progress and settings

Completed lessons, test results, your review schedule, the name you enter and settings such as the theme are stored only in your device's own storage. They are never sent to a server. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Read-aloud

English examples are spoken by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The app does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

Network access

All lesson content ships inside the app. On launch the app downloads a single static configuration file to learn the minimum supported version. No personal data is sent with this request. Without an internet connection the app keeps working normally.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that collects data is ever added, this page and the Google Play data safety declaration will be updated accordingly.
